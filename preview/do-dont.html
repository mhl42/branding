Colour & contrast

Do

Body text on Ink is rgba(250,246,238,.82). Headings only go to full Paper.

Don't

Paper text on Gold. Gold is an accent surface — text on Gold is always Ink.

Do

When a card inverts to Ink, flip every descendant: body, list markers, em/strong, captions, chips, code.

Don't

Override strong or em alone — they inherit the card colour. Override both, or neither.

Decorative gold dot

Do

One dot per card, in chrome — top-right of tiles, threat tiles, channel tiles, code blocks.

Don't

Place the dot over typography. If it would land within 16px of a glyph, drop it.

Logo / lockup

Do

Ship lockup-light.svg + lockup-dark.svg as a pair. Switch on body[data-theme].

Don't

Recolour the lockup with filter: invert(). The tile uses currentColor; theme handles it.

Do

One lockup per slide. The cover carries the wordmark or the periodic tile.

Don't

Pair the wordmark and the tile on the same slide.

Imagery & QR

Do

Hardware / product photos: aspect-ratio: 16/9 + object-fit: contain on an Ink panel.

Don't

Crop product photos with cover. Avatars are fine with cover + 3px Ink border + 6px Gold offset.

Do

QR is a static inline SVG — Paper bg, Ink fg, mono caption below in uppercase tracking.

Don't

Generate QR at runtime, or round the QR's own corners. Round the wrap instead.

Type floors · 1920 × 1080

Do

Body ≥ 24px · eyebrow ≥ 22px · stat numerals 80–96px. text-wrap: pretty on every paragraph.

Don't

Shrink stat numerals to fit. Re-flow the layout instead.
