Read `GUIDELINES.md` first. It holds the voice, the three colours, the typography, the rules for the mark, and the illustration style. `tokens.css` is the source for every colour, size and spacing value. Assets are in `logos/`, `icons/`, `social/`, `illustrations/` and `fonts/`; copy them out rather than recreating them.

Hard rules that are easy to get wrong:

- Three colours only. Gold is one element per surface.
- Inter, weights 400 / 500 / 700 / 800. Sentence case. Mono for identifiers and labels.
- The tile mark is always an outline. Never fill, recolour, rotate or move the gold dot.
- No emoji, no gradients, no shadows on cards. Hairline border and 14px radius is the card.
- Headings are statements, not questions or slogans.
